Privacy Policy — Routine Check

Local-first habit tracker • Ads via Google Mobile Ads

Overview

Routine Check is a simple, timeline-based habit tracker. We keep things private: your routines and completions are stored locally on your device. This page explains how data is handled and how advertising is used.

Last updated: 2026-09-25
Contact: support@greatestinteractive.com

Information we collect

  • Local app data: routines, completion records (dates/times), titles and optional notes. This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Advertising identifiers: We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The AdMob SDK may access device advertising identifiers and signals to serve ads and measure performance.

How we use information

Local data is used only to provide the app's functionality (timelines, streaks, history). Advertising identifiers are used by ad networks to display relevant ads. We do not sell personal data.

Ads & third-party services

We display banner and rewarded ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ad networks may collect device identifiers and other signals. We do not control third-party data practices — see their privacy pages:

  • Google Ad privacy and ad technologies
  • If you have concerns about ads, please contact support at support@greatestinteractive.com.

Children

Routine Check is intended for a general audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's personal information was collected, contact us and we'll take appropriate steps.

Security & data retention

Your data is stored locally on the device. We recommend enabling device-level security (PIN/biometric). Data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes.

If you have questions or privacy requests (data deletion, etc.) email: support@greatestinteractive.com
